About Prophet Samuel Adegboyega

Prophet Samuel Ajayi Adegboyega is a Nigerian pastor, prophet, administrator, author, philanthropist, and Christian leader whose life has been devoted to proclaiming the Gospel of Jesus Christ and raising generations of believers through prayer, holiness, discipleship, and faithful biblical teaching. As the Founder, President, and First General Overseer of the Salvation Proclaimers Anointed Church (SPAC) Worldwide, he has faithfully served God for more than five decades, leading a ministry that has transformed thousands of lives across Nigeria and beyond.

From humble beginnings in a rural village in Ogun State to a distinguished corporate career and an enduring legacy in Christian ministry, his journey is a remarkable testimony of God's grace, unwavering faith, and sacrificial obedience. Through SPAC Worldwide and the humanitarian initiative, Focus for the Future (FFF), he continues to impact lives through spiritual leadership, youth empowerment, education, and community development.

About the Salvation Proclaimers Anointed Church (SPAC)

The Salvation Proclaimers Anointed Church (SPAC) Worldwide is a Pentecostal Christian ministry founded by Prophet Samuel Ajayi Adegboyega and headquartered in Ibadan, Oyo State, Nigeria. The church is committed to proclaiming the message of salvation through Jesus Christ, promoting holiness, prayer, evangelism, prophetic ministry, and the power of the Holy Spirit.

Over the years, SPAC has expanded across Nigeria with branches in cities including Ibadan, Lagos, and Abeokuta, as well as an international presence in the United Kingdom through SPAC Nxtion Family, led by Pastor Tobi Adegboyega. Beyond its spiritual mission, the church is actively involved in youth empowerment, scholarships, welfare support, and community development initiatives aimed at transforming lives and raising future leaders.
